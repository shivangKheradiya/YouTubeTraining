# 📘 02. Expressions

This chapter introduces one of the most fundamental aspects of the C programming language: **expressions**. In C, expressions are more flexible and powerful than in many other languages. They are built from two atomic elements: **data** and **operators**.  

- **Data** can be represented by variables, constants, or values returned by functions.  
- **Operators** provide a wide range of ways to manipulate and combine data.
